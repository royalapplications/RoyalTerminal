# Recovering a client after hot history eviction

Set `ROYALTERMINAL_MUX_COLD_REPLAY_MIB=4` before starting the host to enable a four-MiB **additional logical storage budget per block**. The allowed range is 0–32, default 0. Embedders set `MultiplexerOptions.MaxColdReplayBytes`. This is a process-local, lossless replay archive. It does not store terminal contents on disk or restart a process. See also [the initial protocol](./rex-experimental-implementation.md) and [the parity increments](./rex-parity-continuation.md).

When the hot journal evicts an event, the optional archive retains it. Sealed pages hold up to 64 KiB of output or 256 ordered event headers, and losslessly compress payloads when useful. Incompressible data is kept verbatim. Resize records, including pixel dimensions and rollback messages, stay in order among byte records, as do exit and close. No code converts bytes to strings, approximates a parser snapshot with styled VT, or answers queries during playback.

A reconnect takes a reference snapshot of the cold pages and subscribes to the hot and live stream under the same publication lock. The reader inflates at most one page at a time outside that lock. Historical bytes returned to each subscriber are independently owned. A slow reader may still exceed its hot/live queue limit and must resume explicitly. Readers holding a cold prefix are bounded, including late attachments to an exited process. Disposal drops the unread cold pages without decompressing them. The wire format and `ready` fence are unchanged.

`firstRetainedSequence` is the earliest recoverable event across both tiers. Fresh clients require it to be 1. A client retaining its parser can reconnect when `after >= firstRetainedSequence - 1`. Older requests still receive `HistoryGap`. The cold archive evicts entire oldest pages and caps decoded logical history at 8× the configured budget, up to 128 MiB per block. Metadata incurs a logical charge; this is not an exact CLR heap-size guarantee. Pinned readers retain immutable pages until drained or disposed. Recovery cost is proportional to events replayed. A constant-size, versioned canonical parser checkpoint and recovery after host restart are **not** implemented by this mechanism.

[Replay tests](../../tests/RoyalTerminal.Multiplexing.Tests/ColdReplayTests.cs) compare the entire byte/geometry timeline against a continuously attached observer, seek inside a compressed page, mutate returned buffers, force eviction with seeded noisy bytes, and reattach to an exited process returning 37. Existing history-gap behavior remains the default when the extra retention budget is zero.
